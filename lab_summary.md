This application fetches technology news from NewsAPI and, for each article, generates a summary in Spanish and classifies its sentiment (positive, neutral or negative) using language models. To avoid depending on a single provider, it calls OpenAI first and, if that fails, automatically falls back to Cohere. Each call is retried up to 3 times, waiting 1, 2 and 4 seconds between attempts so as not to overload the provider; errors that retrying cannot fix, such as an invalid key or an exhausted balance, are not retried and the app switches straight to the backup. Cost is controlled by counting the input and output tokens of every call and checking a maximum daily budget before making it. In testing, OpenAI returned a 429 error because of insufficient credit and the application completed the job with Cohere without interruption, at a total cost of about $0.0004 for 3 articles.
